# Privacy Policy

**Effective: July 15, 2026**

This policy describes how SoloPop (the "App") handles your information.
SoloPop is operated by Tin Liu ("we" / "us"). You can reach us at
tliu2930@gmail.com.

The short version: SoloPop helps solo travelers meet up in person. We
collect the minimum we need to make that work, we never sell your data,
and you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from
inside the App at any time.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your
name and email address with us. You may hide your email through Apple's
private relay; in that case we only receive the relay address. We store:

- A unique user identifier
- Your email address (or Apple relay address)

**Profile you create.** To meet other travelers you build a profile. We
store your display name, gender, age, nationality, spoken languages, a
short bio, and the photos you upload. Your age must be 18 or older to
use SoloPop.

**Contact handles.** You may add contact details — phone number,
WhatsApp, email, Telegram, Instagram, and/or WeChat — so matched
travelers can reach you. These stay private until you and another user
are matched (see below).

**Joinables and applications you create.** When you host or apply to a
"joinable" (a meetup), we store its title, plan/description, meeting
location, time, budget, category, group-size and age/gender preferences,
the application messages you send, and the accepted-participant list.

**Location.** With your permission, we use your device location to show
joinables near you and to help you set the meeting location of a joinable
you create. We store the locations of joinables you create, your chosen
base location, and the map area you last viewed (so we can notify you
about new joinables nearby). We do not track your location in the
background.

**Notification preferences and push tokens.** If you opt in to
notifications, we store the push token Apple gives us, along with your
per-device notification preferences, so we can deliver the alerts you've
asked for (such as a new application or an acceptance).

**Safety data.** If you block or report another user or a joinable, we
store that action — who was blocked or reported, and the reason and any
detail you provide — so we can enforce our rules and keep the community
safe.

**Photo verification.** SoloPop checks that a profile photo contains a
face. This check runs entirely on your device; we store only a flag
noting that a given photo passed, never a faceprint or biometric
template.

## 2. How your information is shared with other users

SoloPop is a social app, so some of what you enter is shown to other
people using it:

- Your **profile** — photos, display name, age, nationality, languages,
  and bio — is visible to other travelers when they view a joinable you
  host or an application you send.
- Your **contact handles** are shared with another user **only after an
  application is accepted** by the host — a mutual match. Until then they
  stay private.
- The **joinables** you host are visible to other users who can see them
  on the map and in the Discover feed.

Please share only what you're comfortable with others seeing, and
exchange contact details only with people you trust.

## 3. What we don't collect

- We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't collect biometric or faceprint data (face checks are on-device).
- We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.

## 4. Why we collect it

We use the data above only to operate the App: keep you signed in, show
you relevant nearby joinables, let you host and apply to meetups,
introduce matched travelers, send notifications you've asked for, and
keep the community safe.

We don't profile you, don't build advertising audiences, and don't share
data with marketers.

## 5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("sub-processors") to run the
App:

- **Apple Inc.** — Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service,
  and the App Store. Their handling of your data is governed by Apple's
  own privacy policy.
- **Supabase Inc.** — Hosts our database, file storage (your photos), and
  serverless functions. They store the account, profile, and joinable
  data described above on our behalf. Their handling is governed by
  Supabase's privacy policy.

We do not share your data with anyone else except as required by law
(e.g., a valid court order).

## 6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can
delete it at any time.

### Deleting your account yourself

Open the App, go to the **Profile** tab, then **Delete account →
Delete**. We permanently delete:

- Your authentication record
- Your profile and uploaded photos
- The joinables you host
- Your applications
- Your registered push tokens
- Your block and report records tied to your account

This deletion happens within minutes and cannot be undone. If you'd
prefer to delete your data without using the in-app button, email us at
tliu2930@gmail.com and we'll do it manually.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the
EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA, including the right to access, correct,
delete, or port your data. You can:

- **Access** your data by viewing it in the App.
- **Correct** it by editing your profile in the App or contacting us.
- **Delete** it using the in-app Delete account button.
- **Port** it — email us and we will send you a JSON export.
- **Object** to specific processing, or withdraw consent for
  notifications or location access (via iOS Settings).

EU users have the right to lodge a complaint with their national data
protection authority.

## 8. Children

SoloPop is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an
account, and the App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has
created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. International users

We are based in the United States. If you use the App from outside the
US, your data will be transferred to and processed in the US. Our
sub-processors (Apple and Supabase) operate globally and may process
data in regions including the US and the EU.

## 10. Security

We use HTTPS for all server communication, store data on
encrypted-at-rest cloud infrastructure, and limit access to our backend
to the App's operator. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your
data the way we'd want our own treated. Because SoloPop involves meeting
people in person, please also use common-sense safety: meet in public,
tell someone your plans, and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Effective" date at the
top tracks the latest revision. If we make a material change, we'll
surface it inside the App or notify you in another reasonable way before
the change takes effect.
