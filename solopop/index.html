SoloPop

Meet fellow solo travelers

Coming soon to the App Store

Traveling solo doesn't mean being alone. SoloPop lets you host or join small, local get-togethers — a "joinable" — with other travelers near you: grab dinner, share a day trip, or explore a neighborhood together.

Highlights

📍

Discover what's nearby

See joinables around you on a map. Filter by time, category, and who they're for, and find plans that fit your day.

👥

Host or apply

Post a joinable with a plan, place, and time — or apply to someone else's with a short note. Contact details unlock only when a host accepts.

🛡️

Built for safety

Sign in with Apple, block or report anyone, and agree to community guidelines with zero tolerance for abuse. Reported content is hidden right away.

🔒

Privacy first

Your contact info stays private until you choose to connect. No tracking, no third-party analytics, no data sold. Delete your account anytime.
