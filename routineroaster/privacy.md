# Privacy Policy

**Effective: September 5, 2026**

This policy describes how Routine Roaster (the "App") handles your
information. Routine Roaster is operated by Tin Liu ("we" / "us"). You can
reach us at tliu2930@gmail.com.

The short version: there is no account and no sign-in, so we do not know who
you are. Almost nothing leaves your phone, we never sell your data, and
deleting the App deletes everything it stored.

## 1. What stays on your device

All of this lives only on your phone and is never uploaded:

- Your routines, the days they run, and their start and end dates
- Which routines you completed, and when
- The coaches you write, including their names, descriptions and portraits
- The messages your coach has sent you
- Your notification times and preferences

Deleting the App deletes all of it. We keep no copy and cannot recover it.

## 2. What leaves your device

**Only when you tap "Generate with Pro."** That sends the coach name and the
character description you typed to our server, which passes it to Anthropic
(anthropic.com) to write that coach's messages and choose a portrait. Nothing
else is sent with it — not your routines, not your completion history, and
nothing that identifies you.

Generating a coach **on device** sends nothing at all and works with no network
connection. It is free and unlimited.

## 3. The anonymous identifier

Pro generation costs us money per use, so the App counts how many each install
has used. That requires something to count against, so the App creates a random
identifier the first time it contacts our server.

It is not derived from your device, your Apple ID, your email, or anything about
you. We cannot use it to identify you, contact you, or link you to any other
service. It exists to hold a number.

## 4. Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email, or
payment details.

Apple provides a signed receipt for each purchase, and we store its transaction
identifier so the generations you paid for can be credited — and restored if you
reinstall or move to a new phone. That identifier records a purchase, not a
person.

## 5. Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent through
a notification server, and no push token is created or transmitted.

## 6. What we do not do

- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No third-party SDKs in the App
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone, for any purpose

## 7. Retention

The anonymous identifier, the count of generations used, and purchase
transaction identifiers are retained while the App is in service, because they
are what prevent a paid balance from being lost. Coach descriptions sent for
generation are not retained after the coach has been written.

## 8. Children

Routine Roaster is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that
could identify a child.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Effective" date above changes
with it.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email **tliu2930@gmail.com**.
