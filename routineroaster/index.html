Routine Roaster

A habit tracker with an opinion

Set your routines, pick the days they run, then check them off. That part is ordinary. What isn't is the coach who notices — every morning they tell you what's on, and the next day they deliver a verdict on yesterday.

Highlights

🔥

Five coaches, five tones

Disappointed but loving. Gentle and quietly keeping score. Hyping you up. Treating you as a resource. Or actively rooting for your failure.

✍️

Write your own

Describe any character in a sentence and they get a voice and a face. A retired drill sergeant. A cat that's sweet but cold. Your future self, disappointed.

📊

Thirty days, day by day

Completion rate, current and best streak, and a week-aligned grid so you can see which weekday you always miss.

🔒

No account, ever

No sign-in, nothing to log into. Your routines, history and coaches stay on your phone. No tracking, no analytics, no data sold.
