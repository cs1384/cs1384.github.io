Accomply

Personal and shared routines

Download on the App Store →

Accomply turns recurring tasks into routines that stick. Track the chores, habits, and check-ins that matter — alone, or shared with the people you live and work with.

Highlights

🗓

Daily, weekly, monthly

Routines auto-reset at the period boundary, so completed items reappear the next day, Monday, or 1st of the month — ready to be done again.

👥

Shared routine groups

Promote any routine group to a shared one and invite others with a share code. Everyone sees who acted on what.

🌱

A companion that grows with you

Each completion sends a star toward your routine companion, leveling it up across 27 stages.

🔒

Privacy by design

Sign in with Apple. Personal routines never leave your device. No tracking, no third-party analytics, no data sold.
