---# Privacy Policy

**Effective: May 11, 2026**

This policy describes how Accomply (the "App") handles your information.
Accomply is operated by Tin Liu ("we" / "us"). You can reach us at
tliu2930@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect the minimum we need to make the App work,
we never sell your data, and you can permanently delete your account
and all associated data from inside the App at any time.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Apple, Apple shares
your name and email address with us. You may hide your email through
Apple's private relay; in that case we only receive the relay address.
We store:

- A unique user identifier
- Your display name (provided by Apple, or a default fallback if Apple
  does not share one)
- Your subscription tier and its expiration date, if you've purchased
  Accomply Pro

**Routine and group data you create.** Routine titles, notes,
recurrence (daily / weekly / monthly), completion status, completion
timestamps, and group names and colors.

- *Personal* routines stay on your device only and are never transmitted
  to our servers.
- *Shared* routine groups are stored on our servers so they can sync
  between members of the group.

**Notification preferences.** Whether you've enabled shared-group
notifications. Stored against your account so the same preference
applies on every device you sign in on.

**Push notification tokens.** If you opt in to notifications, we store
the token Apple gives us so we can deliver push notifications to your
devices.

**Subscription metadata.** If you subscribe to Accomply Pro, we
receive from Apple the original transaction identifier, product
identifier, and expiration date so we can recognize you as a Pro
subscriber. We do not receive your payment method, billing address,
or any financial information — Apple handles all payments.

**Calendar events (optional).** If you grant calendar access, the App
reads your event titles and times *on your device* to display them
alongside your routines. Calendar data is never transmitted to our
servers.

## 2. What we don't collect

- We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't collect your location.
- We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.

## 3. Why we collect it

We use the data above only to operate the App: keep you signed in,
sync your shared routines between devices, send notifications you've
asked for, and recognize whether you've subscribed.

We don't profile you, don't build advertising audiences, and don't
share data with marketers.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("sub-processors") to run
the App:

- **Apple Inc.** — Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service,
  the App Store, and StoreKit. Their handling of your data is governed
  by Apple's own privacy policy.
- **Supabase Inc.** — Hosts our database and serverless functions.
  They store the routine and account data described above on our
  behalf. Their handling is governed by Supabase's privacy policy.

We do not share your data with anyone else except as required by law
(e.g., a valid court order).

## 5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You
can ask us to delete it at any time, and we will automatically delete
accounts that go unused for a long period.

### Deleting your account yourself

Open the App, tap the **person icon** in the top toolbar, then
**Delete Account → Delete**. We permanently delete:

- Your authentication record
- Your profile
- Routine groups you own
- Routines you authored
- Your memberships in shared groups
- Your registered push tokens

This deletion happens within minutes. Records that Apple keeps for
its own tax and billing purposes are outside our control.

If you'd prefer to delete your data without using the in-app button,
email us at tliu2930@gmail.com and we'll do it manually.

### Inactive accounts

If you don't sign in to Accomply for **11 months**, we send a push
notification to your registered devices warning that your account
will be deleted. You then have **30 days** — for a total of 12 months
of inactivity — to open the App and keep your account active. Simply
signing in resets the clock; we never delete your account in the
middle of you actively using it.

After 12 months of continuous inactivity, your account and all of
the data listed above are permanently deleted. This runs once a day.

**Active Pro subscribers are exempt** from inactivity deletion. As
long as your subscription is active and unexpired, we will not delete
your account, regardless of how recently you opened the App.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as
the EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA, including the right to access,
correct, delete, or port your data. You can:

- **Access** your data by viewing it in the App.
- **Correct** it by editing it in the App or contacting us.
- **Delete** it using the in-app Delete Account button.
- **Port** it — email us and we will send you a JSON export.
- **Object** to specific processing, or withdraw consent for
  notifications.

EU users have the right to lodge a complaint with their national
data protection authority.

## 7. Children

Accomply is rated 4+ on the App Store but is not directed at children
under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you
believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we
will delete it.

## 8. International users

We are based in the United States. If you use the App from outside
the US, your data will be transferred to and processed in the US. Our
sub-processors (Apple and Supabase) operate globally and may process
data in regions including the US and the EU.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS for all server communication, store data on
encrypted-at-rest cloud infrastructure, and limit access to our
backend to the App's operator. No system is perfectly secure, but we
treat your data the way we'd want our own treated.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Effective" date
at the top tracks the latest revision. If we make a material change,
we'll surface it inside the App or notify you in another reasonable
way before the change takes effect.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email **tliu2930@gmail.com**.
